DATA NOTES

What these numbers mean

ProjectionsWeekly projections are supplied by Sleeper for the selected scoring format; Blitz does not generate them.

Recent and season averagesRecent averages use completed games before the selected week. Season average is Sleeper’s latest season-to-date result.

Opponent and kickoffScheduled matchup, venue side, and game time come from ESPN. Bye-week players show no scheduled game.

Unavailable metricsDefense-vs-position ranks, projection floor/ceiling, ownership, and league waiver status are omitted when the feeds do not provide them.

Data: Sleeper API projections, player directory, and weekly stats; ESPN NFL schedule. Provider availability may vary.
